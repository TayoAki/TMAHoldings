# 10 · Landing the first business: sell a service first

"You start by serving them. You earn their trust, and eventually you own one."

Pick one industry, sell those firms one annoying job done with agents, learn how the firms
work from the inside while building the agents on real work, and after 6–12 months of good
work one of the owners will be ready to step back. You'll be the obvious person to take over.

## Step 1: pick the industry (week 1)
- Score candidates with the industry table in `thesis/THESIS.md` (licensing hurdle, recurring
  revenue, share of agent-ready work, deal supply near you, your edge).
- Run the `niche-validator` skill on the wedge offer, e.g. "AI month-end cleanup for bookkeeping
  firms". Gate 0 still applies: buyers exist, competitors price it publicly, 50%+ gross margin,
  and you can name where the owners gather.

## Step 2: design one offer (weeks 1–2)
- One job, not a platform: **month-end cleanup for a firm's 5 messiest clients**, or **chasing
  missing documents** before tax season.
- `offer-architect` skill: fixed scope, fixed price, fast turnaround, a real guarantee. Example
  shape: "We close your five messiest clients each month, reconciled to the bank, reviewed by
  a person, back to you by the 5th. Flat fee per client file."
- Your delivery **is this repo**: set up each client firm as a business folder in assisted
  mode, where *their* staff (or you) approve every deliverable. Every job trains the agents and
  adds to the corrections log.

## Step 3: find the first firms (weeks 2–8)
- `icp-canvas` → owners of $500k–$3M firms in your region, especially owners 55+.
- `lead-source-planner` → association directories, local CPA and banker referrals, direct email
  from a real person (you). Talk about their staff's workload, not about AI.

## Step 4: deliver and listen (months 2–12)
- Deliver on time, every time. Quality beats features.
- Quarterly check-in with each owner: how's the team, what's next for you, have you thought
  about succession? Keep notes in your CRM; they are your deal pipeline.
- Every correction your reviewers make goes in the log; every Wednesday you turn repeats into
  rules. By month 6 you will have a rules file no competitor has.

## Gates (don't skip)
| Gate | Pass when | If not |
|---|---|---|
| W1 · Paying firms | 3 firms paying for the wedge within 90 days | change the offer or the industry (demand-first Gate 2) |
| W2 · Agents earn trust | drafts needing fixes under 20% on the wedge job for 8 weeks | keep tuning rules before selling more |
| W3 · Succession signal | 2+ owners have discussed succession with you | keep serving; widen the list |
| W4 · Deal ready | financing plan confirmed with a lender; buy box final; one target screened | runbook 08 |

The wedge business also pays the bills while you wait: it's a real service business, not a
marketing expense.
