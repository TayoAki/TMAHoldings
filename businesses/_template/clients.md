# Clients — REPLACE: Business name

Who the clients are, what they care about, and who they are loyal to. Agents read this
before drafting anything for a client. Keep the roster (dates, status, fee) in `clients.csv`;
keep the judgment here.

For each client, capture in the day-one interview:
- How long they have been a client, and who they are loyal to (a person, not the firm).
- What makes them sensitive (fees, late reports, a past mistake).
- Their quirks: report dates, formats, who to copy, how they like to be addressed.
- What would make them leave.
